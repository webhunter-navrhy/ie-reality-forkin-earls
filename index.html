Set against the spectacular natural backdrop of Avoca, this exceptional five-bedroom detached residence is a beautifully appointed family home where contemporary design, exquisite finishes and breathtaking countryside surroundings come together.

Nestled amongst mature woodland and rolling countryside, the property enjoys an enviable sense of privacy and tranquillity. The interiors have been finished to an exceptionally high standard, with a sophisticated modern aesthetic and a bright, open feel throughout. Generous proportions, carefully considered finishes, and an abundance of natural light create an elegant yet welcoming home.

This property boasts an impressive kitchen and living space, where a striking picture window perfectly frames the surrounding landscape, bringing the beauty of Avoca directly into the home. The open-plan design creates a wonderful space for both everyday family life and entertaining, with the countryside providing an ever-changing backdrop.

A particularly special feature of the property is its connection with the outdoors. Double doors on both floors open out towards the stunning views, allowing the scenery to become an integral part of the home and creating beautiful spaces from which to enjoy the peaceful woodland and countryside setting.

The home has been thoughtfully arranged with the principal living accommodation positioned on the upper floor, maximising the spectacular elevated views and natural light, while the bedrooms are situated on the lower level, creating a peaceful and private retreat.

Accommodation is substantial, comprising five bedrooms and two bathrooms, offering excellent flexibility for family living, guests or those seeking dedicated home-working space.

Outside, the house is enveloped by the landscape for which Avoca and the Garden of Ireland are so renowned. Mature trees, woodland and open countryside create a wonderfully private setting and an exceptional sense of escape from everyday life.

Combining contemporary interiors, impressive proportions and an extraordinary countryside setting, this is a truly special home offering modern luxury surrounded by the natural beauty of Avoca.
